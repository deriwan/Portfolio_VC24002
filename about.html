My Story

Who Am I?

My name is Deriwan Akhmal Bin Mohd Nasir, and I am from Bandar Puncak Alam, Selangor. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Industrial Electronic Automation with Honours at Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA).

My interest in electronics began during my vocational education at Kolej Vokasional Datuk Seri Mohd Zin. Through academic projects and practical training, I developed an interest in automation systems, electronics, instrumentation and troubleshooting.

My technical experience includes quality control and ESD compliance at Ericsson, along with CCTV installation, structured cabling and networking tasks during my industrial training at Hafiz Safety & Security Services.

Outside the classroom, my leadership experience as a Commanding Officer in Pasukan Kadet Maritim Malaysia (PASKAM) helped me improve my communication, teamwork and decision-making skills.
